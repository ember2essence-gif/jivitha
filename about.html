About us

A community-led group focused on learning, connection and local responses to climate change.

History

In 2024, Whittlesea Climate Action Network and Mernda Neighbourhood House initiated a local community climate resilience project with an important question:

Through conversations and local engagement, community members from diverse backgrounds came together to share their thoughts, values, concerns, and hopes. Those early discussions laid the foundation for the Whittlesea Climate Action Network (WCAN), a community-led group focused on learning, connection, and local responses to climate change. WCAN was formally established in December 2024 and continues to meet regularly.

About us

The Whittlesea Climate Action Network (WCAN) is a local community group that aims to raise awareness of, and deepen understanding of, the current impacts of climate change within the Municipality of City of Whittlesea, while fostering a culture of sustainable action and advocacy to protect our ecosystems, environment, and future.

What began in 2024 with 10 community members, after discovering that no climate action groups were operating specifically for City of Whittlesea, has steadily grown into a network of more than 40 dedicated members.

To realise our vision of an informed and prepared community, WCAN also collaborates with local groups and organisations to strengthen awareness, capacity, and mutual support. Our connections with Friends of the Earth, Melbourne Arboretum, Mernda Neighbourhood House, Whittlesea Community Connection, City of Whittlesea, and groups such as the Friends of Edgar's Creek and Lalor Landcare are helping create a strong collective voice. Through these partnerships and our diverse group of passionate community members, we believe we can make meaningful progress toward building a sustainable and connected society.

WCAN is building a community-led response to climate change in the City of Whittlesea. Through small focus groups, we make sure every voice is heard. At larger events like Climate Convergence, we connect with the wider community. This helps us respond to both individual needs and neighbourhood priorities with equal care.
