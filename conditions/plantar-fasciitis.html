Condition

Plantar Fasciitis

Recovery: Typically 4–8 weeks with consistent stretching and treatment

Symptoms

  • Stabbing heel pain with the first steps of the day
  • Pain that reduces with activity but returns after rest
  • Tenderness along the arch of the foot

Causes

  • Repetitive strain on the plantar fascia
  • Tight calf and foot muscles
  • Prolonged standing, running, or improper footwear

How We Treat It

  • Calf and plantar fascia stretching
  • Manual therapy and soft tissue release
  • Strengthening of foot and ankle muscles
  • Guidance on supportive footwear

Recovery Time

Typically 4–8 weeks with consistent stretching and treatment; longstanding cases may take longer

Why Choose Jivitha Revive

  • Evidence-based stretching and loading protocols
  • Attention to underlying causes, not just the painful spot
  • Practical daily routines that fit real schedules
